What it actually does

01

Grouping that stays out of the way

Tabs sort themselves by domain, subdomain, IP address or rules you write with regex.

02

An agent, not a chatbot

Ask in plain language and it acts on the browser itself.

03

Deep work, measured

A floating Pomodoro with tasks grouped by project, and a dashboard of what you got done.

04

And the rest of the toolbox

Two tabs side by side. Reading modes, a theme creator, a cookie editor, screenshots, QR codes.

Put the mouse down

Press one key and every link on the page grows a label.

  • f label every link
  • gg jump to the top
  • CtrlShiftZ fold this group
  • AltShiftP open the panel

Where your data goes

Nowhere.

There is no account, and no server of ours behind any of this.

Two people build this

It is free, there are no ads, nothing is tracked and nothing is sold.
