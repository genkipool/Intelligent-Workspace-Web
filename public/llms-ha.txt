# Intelligent Workspace

> Intelligent Workspace wata ingantacciyar manhaja ce ta ƙarin bincike (browser extension) mai tushe a kan Manifest V3 da kuma yanayi na aiki mai inganci wanda aka gina da Svelte, Vite, da CRXJS. Tana tafiyar da tsara shafukan yanar gizo (tabs) cikin fasaha, tattara shafuka ta atomatik ko bisa ƙa'idoji, gudanar da wakilan AI masu cin gashin kansu (tana goyon bayan Google Gemini Cloud API da kuma Chrome Built-in Prompt API), kewayawa cikin shafi ta salon Vim don bin hanyoyin yanar gizo (links), tsarin umarnin Omnibar mai prefixes 41, ɗaukar bayanan kula ta hanyoyi da dama (cikakken rubutu, lissafin duba ayyuka, Kanban, zane-zanen Mermaid, dabarun lissafi na KaTeX), ɗaukar hotuna da faifan bidiyo masu inganci da fasahar WASM OCR a cikin manhaja, ƙididdigar lokaci ta Pomodoro tare da ƙararrawar da ba ta da kuskuren lokaci da kunna sauti a ɓoye, da kuma auna ayyukan yanar gizo ta Declarative Net Request (DNR), kayyade lokacin shafuka, da toshe shafukan yanar gizo.

## 1. Tushen Tsarin Gine-gine da Yanayin Gudanarwa

Wannan ƙarin manhaja yana aiki ne a ƙarƙashin tsarin Manifest V3 na Google Chrome, yana raba ayyuka masu ci gaba tsakanin Service Worker na bayan fage, fuskokin UI da aka killace a keɓe, takardun sauti na wajen allo, da kuma rubutun abubuwan ciki (content scripts) masu yawo a cikin shafi:

1. **Tsarin Manifest (`manifest.json`)**:
   - **Izini (abubuwa 21)**: `tabs`, `tabGroups`, `storage`, `favicon`, `contextMenus`, `notifications`, `sidePanel`, `scripting`, `downloads`, `downloads.open`, `system.display`, `declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess`, `cookies`, `history`, `sessions`, `bookmarks`, `readingList`, `clipboardWrite`, `alarms`, `offscreen`, da `idle`.
   - **Izinin Masauki (Host Permissions)**: `["<all_urls>"]`.
   - **Service Worker**: `src/core/background.js` yana amfani da saukar da kayayyaki a lokaci guda ta `importScripts`.
   - **Aikin Kayan Aiki (Toolbar Action)**: Da gangan aka bar `default_popup`. Tsarin yana tilasta `chrome.action.setPopup({ popup: '' })`, yana tabbatar da cewa `chrome.action.onClicked` yana aiki nan take kuma yana buɗe `chrome.sidePanel` zuwa fuskar da mai amfani ya maƙala ba tare da jinkirin fara buɗe popup ba.
   - **Kalmar Sirri ta Omnibox**: Kalmar da aka yi rajista ita ce `"find"`.
   - **Umarni**: Gajerun hanyoyin maɓallai 24 na matakin tsarin kwamfuta da aka yi wa rajista a ƙarƙashin `commands`.
   - **Ƙa'idar Tsaron Abun Ciki**: `script-src 'self' 'wasm-unsafe-eval'; object-src 'self';`.

2. **Tsaron Sadarwa Tsakanin Hanyoyin Aiki**:
   - `src/core/background/messaging.js` yana tace saƙonni daga rubutun abubuwan ciki (content scripts) bisa wani sahihin jerin sunaye da aka amince da su (`ALLOWED_CONTENT_SCRIPT_ACTIONS`). Shafukan yanar gizo marasa izini ba za su iya kiran masu rubuta ma'ajiya ta ciki, Chrome APIs na kowane iri, ko masu canza ƙa'idoji ba.

3. **Duniyoyin Aiwatarwa Biyu**:
   - Rubutun abubuwan ciki suna aiki ne a cikin duniyoyi biyu: `ISOLATED` (fannin ƙarin manhaja) da kuma `MAIN` (fannin aiwatar da shafi). Rubuce-rubuce kamar `allowRightClickHook.js`, `videoPipHook.js`, `youtubePreviewAudioHook.js`, da `youtubeShortsFeedHook.js` suna aiki a cikin `MAIN` don tare hanyoyin prototype, sauya bayanan kaddarori, da kula da abubuwan da ke faruwa na musamman kai tsaye.

## 2. Sashe na 1: Gudanar da Ƙa'idoji (Gestionar Reglas)

Tsarin Gudanar da Ƙa'idoji yana tsara shafuka zuwa rukunoni ta atomatik bisa ƙayyadaddun sharuɗɗan da mai amfani ya bayyana.

### 2.1 Tsarin Bayanai (`CustomRule`)
Kowace ƙa'ida ta musamman tana bin wannan tsari:
```typescript
interface CustomRule {
  name: string;        // Haruffa 1 zuwa 16. Kada ya ƙunshi kowane sarari (whitespace).
  color: RuleColor;    // Ɗaya daga cikin launuka 9: 'grey' | 'blue' | 'red' | 'yellow' | 'green' | 'pink' | 'purple' | 'cyan' | 'orange'
  urls: string[];      // Jerin sahihan URLs, yankuna (domains), ko sunayen masauki (hostnames).
  active: boolean;     // Babban maɓallin kunnawa ko kashe duba ƙa'ida.
  isStarred?: boolean; // Alamar zaɓi da ke nuna ƙa'ida a matsayin wacce aka fi so don fifita ta a jerin UI.
}
```

### 2.2 Tsarin Fifiko
Lokacin da `groupTabs()` a cikin `src/core/background/groupManager.js` ke sarrafa shafuka, ana duba shafukan ne a kan matakan rukuni bisa tsari mai tsauri:
1. **Ƙa'idoji na Musamman (`applyCustomRules`)**: Yana duba ƙa'idojin da ke aiki bisa jerin bayyanarsu ko fifikon mai amfani. Da zarar an rarraba su, ana yin rijistar lambobin ID na shafukan a cikin `groupedTabIds` kuma a cire su daga matakai na gaba.
2. **Rukunonin Tsarin Kwamfuta na Musamman**: Ana tare adiresoshin URL na tsarin kwamfuta tare da sanya su a cikin rukunoni masu launuka na musamman:
   - `chrome://` da shafukan ciki na manhajar bincike
   - `file:///` fayilolin faifan kwamfuta na gida
   - `chrome-extension://` shafukan ƙarin manhajoji
   - IP Addresses da Localhost (`localhost`, `127.0.0.1`, `[::1]`, rundunonin IPv4/IPv6)
3. **Tattara Yankuna (Domain Clustering)**: Shafukan da ke da yanki ɗaya ana haɗa su wuri guda idan yawan shafukan ya kai ko ya zarce `domainThreshold` (asali: shafuka 2).
4. **Tattara Ƙananan Yankuna (Subdomain Clustering)**: Idan an kunna shi, shafukan da ke cikin yanki ɗaya ana raba su bisa ƙananan yankuna na musamman (misali, `docs.github.com` daban da `gist.github.com`).
5. **Rukunin Sauran Abubuwa ("Misc")**: Shafukan da suka rage waɗanda ba su shiga ko'ina ba kuma ba su kai adadin da ake buƙata ba ana sanya su a rukunin `Misc` (wurin sanyawa yana da zaɓi: farko `start`, ƙarshe `end`, ko bin haruffa `alpha`).

### 2.3 Daidaita URL da Ma'anar Daidaitawa
Injin daidaitawa (`matchesRule(tabUrl, ruleUrl)` a cikin `src/core/background/utils.js`) yana amfani da ingantaccen tsarin daidaitawa:
- **Rashin Nuna Bambancin Tsarin Sadarwa (Scheme Agnostic)**: Ana ɗaukar hanyoyin sadarwa na HTTP da HTTPS a matsayin abu ɗaya (`http://example.com` yana daidaita da `https://example.com`). Hanyoyin da ba na yanar gizo ba (`file://`, `chrome://`) suna buƙatar daidaituwar tsari ta ainihi.
- **Daidaita Sunan Masauki**: Ana cire harafin farko na `www.` (`www.github.com` yana komawa `github.com`). Ana mayar da sunayen masauki zuwa ƙananan haruffa kuma suna goyon bayan sunayen yanki na ƙasa da ƙasa (IDNs) ta hanyar punycode.
- **Kula da Lambar Ƙofa (Port Sensitivity)**: Idan wata ƙa'ida ta ƙayyade lambar ƙofa (misali, `localhost:3000`), dole ne shafin ya dace da ainihin wannan lambar ƙofar.
- **Daidaita Hanyar Shafi (Path Matching)**: Ana cire alamar raba hanya ta ƙarshe. Idan wata ƙa'ida ta ƙayyade wata hanya (misali, `example.com/docs`), tana daidaita da shafukan da ke da wannan ainihin hanyar KO ƙananan hanyoyi (`example.com/docs/api`), amma ba ta dace da hanyoyin gefe ba (`example.com/blog`).
- **Daidaita Tambaya da Alamar Hash**: Idan wata ƙa'ida ta ƙunshi rubutun tambaya a bayyane (`?page=1`) ko alamar hash (`#section`), dole ne shafin ya dace da waɗannan sharuɗɗan dalla-dalla.
- **Kariyar Lambobin Dword (`ruleValidation.js`)**: Mai binciken yana ƙin karɓar lambobi zalla (misali, `3242342424`) waɗanda masu fassara URL na WHATWG ke ɗauka a matsayin ingantattun lambobin IPv4 integer DWORDs, yana hana rikicewar rarraba rukuni cikin kuskure.

### 2.4 Rarraba Saitunan Gudanar da Ƙa'idoji
Mai Gudanar da Ƙa'idoji yana ba da cikakkun kayan sarrafa halaye da saituna:
- **Zaɓin Hanyar Ma'ajiya**: Masu amfani za su iya tura ma'ajiyar ƙa'ida tsakanin `chrome.storage.sync` (don daidaita saituna a duk na'urorin da aka shiga da asusun Google, bisa ƙayyadaddun ma'aunin Chrome sync na jimillar 100 KB da 8 KB ga kowane abu) da kuma `chrome.storage.local` (don samun ma'ajiya marar iyaka da ta dace da manyan rukunonin ƙa'idoji masu ɗauke da ɗaruruwan tsare-tsare).
- **Babban Maɓallin Tattara Shafuka da Ƙa'idojin Adadi**:
  - `toggle-clustering`: Babban gajeriyar hanyar maɓalli da maɓallin UI na kunnawa ko kashe tattara yankuna/ƙananan yankuna ta atomatik ba tare da kashe ƙa'idojin da mai amfani ya ƙirƙira ba.
  - `domainThreshold`: Mafi ƙarancin shafukan da ake buƙata daga yanki ɗaya don ƙirƙirar rukunin shafuka ta atomatik (asali: shafuka 2). Ana iya daidaita shi daga 1 zuwa 50.
  - `subdomainThreshold`: Mafi ƙarancin shafukan da ake buƙata don ƙananan yankuna kafin a ƙirƙiri rukunoni na musamman a keɓe.
  - Maɓallin sauya ƙananan yankuna (`toggle-subdomain-grouping`): Yana bambanta ƙananan yankuna masu zaman kansu (misali, `news.ycombinator.com` daban da `ycombinator.com`).
- **Shirya Rukunoni ta Haruffan Haruffa ta Automatik (`toggle-sort-alpha`)**: Injin jera abubuwa nan take da ke tilasta bin tsarin haruffan haruffa a duk rukunonin shafukan da ke buɗe a cikin mashigin shafukan manhaja (`Ctrl+Shift+S` / `Cmd+Shift+S`).
- **Alamomin Hali na Gani (`toggle-prefixes`)**: Alamomin emoji da ake sakawa a farkon sunayen rukunoni don nuna halin bincikensu:
  - `🔒` (Kulle): Rukunin yana ɗauke da shafukan da ba a ziyarta ba kuma ba a karanta ba.
  - `🔍` (Madubin Dubawa): Rukunin yana ɗauke da shafukan da aka ziyarta wani ɓangare nasu.
  - `🗝️` (Mabuɗi Buɗaɗɗe): Dukkan shafukan da ke cikin rukunin an buɗe su kuma an duba su.
  - `⚠️` (Gargaɗi): An gano karo na sunan rukuni iri ɗaya da wani rukunin a wata taga daban.
- **Naɗe Rukunoni Masu Barci ta Automatik (`toggle-collapse-timer`)**: Yana naɗe rukunoni ta atomatik idan ba a amfani da su don tsabtace mashigin shafuka.
- **Babban Dakatarwar Rarraba Rukuni (`feature_pauseGrouping`)**: Babban maɓallin daskarewa wanda ke dakatar da duk ayyukan rarraba rukuni ta atomatik na wucin gadi (na dacewar ƙa'idoji da na tattara shafuka) a lokacin ayyukan bincike inda ya kamata shafuka su kasance a tsarin da mai amfani ya zaɓa.
- **Rarraba Shafi Guda ɗaya zuwa Rukuni (`featureSingleTabGrouping`)**: Yana ba wa ƙa'idoji na musamman damar ƙirƙirar rukunin shafi nan take don shafi guda ɗaya da ya dace, yana tsallake ƙa'idar neman shafuka da yawa.
- **Ƙaramar Fuska (`toggle-compact-mode`)**: Yana rage faɗin kan shafi a cikin mashigin shafukan manhaja ta hanyar nuna harafi guda ɗaya kawai a matsayin alama ta sunan rukuni.
- **Fifiko ta Jawo da Saukewa**: Sake tsara ƙa'idoji a jerin `Rules.svelte` yana canza fifikon bincikensu nan take. Ƙa'idojin da ke sama ana fara duba su tukuna.
- **Bincike Mai Sauƙi (Fuzzy Search)**: Tace bayanai nan take a cikin Mai Gudanar da Ƙa'idoji da Omnibar yana ba da damar gano ƙa'idoji cikin sauri ta taken suna ko hanyoyin URL da suka dace.
- **Shigarwa / Fitarwar JSON**:
  - Yanayin `add`: Yana haɗa ƙa'idojin da aka shigo da su cikin tarin da ake da su, yana cire kwafi.
  - Yanayin `overwrite`: Yana canza dukkan ƙa'idojin da ke aiki gaba ɗaya da sabbin bayanan da aka shigo da su bayan an tabbatar da ingancinsu.
- **Ƙirƙirar Ƙa'ida Cikin Sauri**: Ƙirƙirar ƙa'idoji na musamman da dannawa ɗaya kai tsaye daga manyan foldojin alamun shafi na Chrome ko tarin yankuna na ƙananan rukunoni.
- **Cire Kwafin Ƙa'idoji (`feature_v100_dedup_add_to_rule`)**: Yana cire kwafin hanyoyin URL ta atomatik yayin ƙara sabbin URLs ko yankuna zuwa ƙa'idojin da ake da su, yana hana hauhawar ƙa'idoji da bincike marar amfani.

### 2.5 Ayyuka da Adanawa (`mutateRulesAndSync`)
- **Tsakiyar Sauye-sauye**: Dukkan gyare-gyaren ƙa'idoji suna bi ta `mutateRulesAndSync(mutateFn, sendResponse)` a cikin `src/core/background/handlers/rules.js`. Wannan yana ba da tabbacin daidaita ma'ajiya, sabunta bayanan cikin ƙwaƙwalwa (`extensionSettings.customRules`), sanarwar UI (`rulesUpdated`), da sake rarraba shafuka ta atomatik (`groupTabs()`).
- **Tabbatarwa Nan Take**: Binciken tabbatarwa na ainihin lokaci yana duba keɓantaccen suna, tsayi (haruffa 1-16), ƙuntatawar haruffa, da ingantaccen tsarin URL kafin adanawa.

### 2.6 Hanyoyin Kira
- **Shafin Mai Gudanar da Ƙa'idoji (`Rules.svelte`)**: Ana iya shiga ta cikakken shafin bincike ko a ɓangaren gefe (`?view=rules` / `pg`). Yana ba da cikakken tsarin jawo da saukewa, zaɓin launi a layi, alamun URL, da maɓallan kunna ƙa'idoji.
- **Umarnin Omnibar**:
  - `rl:` — Bincika ƙa'idoji kuma buɗe adiresoshin URL da ke da alaƙa.
  - `cr: <name> [, url1, url2]` — Ƙirƙiri sabuwar ƙa'ida kai tsaye daga mashigin umarni.
  - `atcr:` — Ƙara yanki ko URL na shafin da ke aiki zuwa ƙa'idar da ake da ita.
  - `atr:` — Ƙara buɗaɗɗun shafukan da aka zaɓa ko adiresoshin URL na hannu zuwa ƙa'idar da ake da ita.
  - `ccr:` — Canza launi na wata ƙa'ida ta musamman.
  - `dr:` — Goge ƙa'idoji ko takamaiman URLs daga wata ƙa'ida.
  - `er:` — Gyara ko canza sunan wata ƙa'ida da ake da ita.
- **Zaɓuɓɓukan Danna Dama (Context Menus)**:
  - Danna dama a kan shafi -> "Crear Regla para esta Página" -> "Añadir Raíz del Sitio" / "Añadir URL Completa".
  - Danna dama a kan shafi -> "Añadir a Regla Existente" -> Zaɓi ƙa'ida -> "Añadir Raíz del Sitio" / "Añadir URL Completa".
  - Danna dama a kan shafi -> "Reglas" -> Kunna ko kashe ƙa'ida, buɗe duk adiresoshin URL na ƙa'idar, ko rufe shafukan da suka dace.
- **Gajerun Hanyoyin Maɓallai**:
  - `Alt+R`: Buɗe Mai Gudanar da Ƙa'idoji.
  - `Alt+M`: Kunna ko kashe aikin duk ƙa'idoji a lokaci guda (`toggle-all-rules`).

## 3. Sashe na 2: Jerin Rukunoni da Ayyuka (Listar Grupos)

Fuskar Jerin Rukunoni tana ba da wuri mai sauƙin aiki don dubawa, sarrafawa, da sake tsara rukunonin shafukan manhaja da shafuka ɗai-ɗaiku.

### 3.1 Tsarin Fuskar Mai Amfani (UI)
- **Tarin Abubuwan Ciki**: An gina shi da `ListGroup.svelte`, `GroupCard.svelte`, `GroupActions.svelte`, `TabItem.svelte`, da `TabActions.svelte`.
- **Siffofin Kati**: Yana nuna taken rukuni, alamar launi, adadin shafuka, maɓallin naɗe/buɗe accordion, maƙala kati, da rukunin maɓallan ayyuka da za a iya daidaitawa.
- **Abubuwan Shafi**: Yana nuna alamar favicon ta shafi tare da launi na daban idan babu, taken shafi, alamar sauti mai jiwuwa, alamar kashe sauti, alamar nuna shafi mai aiki, da ayyukan kowane shafi (rufe, yi kwafi, maƙala, raba, watsar).

### 3.2 Yanayi da Halayyar Rukunoni
- **Sarrafa Accordion**: Yanayin buɗewa da naɗewa cikin sauƙi. Yanayin naɗe abubuwa ta atomatik yana naɗe rukunoni marasa aiki idan an buɗe sabon rukuni.
- **Lokacin Naɗe Rukunoni Masu Barci ta Automatik**:
  - Yana naɗe rukunonin shafuka marasa aiki ta atomatik don rage cunkoson gani.
  - Matakan lokaci biyu mabanbanta: Rukunonin da ba sa aiki suna naɗewa bayan minti 1 na rashin aiki (`INACTIVITY_THRESHOLD_INACTIVE_GROUP`), yayin da rukunin da ke aiki yake naɗewa bayan mintuna 15 na rashin aiki (`INACTIVITY_THRESHOLD_ACTIVE_GROUP`). Ana iya daidaita shi daga mintuna 0 zuwa 1440.
- **Canza Suna a Layi da Lokacin Dakatawa**:
  - Masu amfani za su iya canza sunan rukunoni kai tsaye a cikin mashigin shafukan manhaja ko a fuskar UI.
  - `GROUP_NAMING_GRACE_MS` (5000ms) yana dakatar da sake rarraba rukuni ta atomatik yayin da mai amfani ke gyara rubutu, yana hana goge take a tsakiyar rubutu.
  - Ana cire sarari marar faɗi (`\u200B`, `\u200C`, `\u200D`, `\u2060`, `\uFEFF`) yayin bincike.
  - Canza sunan rukunin da aka ƙirƙira ta atomatik yana mayar da shi zuwa rukunin da mai amfani ya sa wa suna, yana sabunta ƙa'idar da ta dace ko mai da shi na dindindin.
- **Alamomin Farko na Rukunoni**:
  - Yana sanya alamomin hali a gaba: `🔒` (ba a ziyarta ba), `🔍` (wani ɓangare), `🗝️` (an duba duka), `⚠️` (karo da wani).
- **Sake Tsari ta Jawo da Saukewa**: An haɗa shi da `sortable.js` don sake tsara shafuka a cikin rukunoni, matsar da shafuka tsakanin rukunoni, ko sake tsara katin rukunoni.
- **Siffar Rukunoni Masu Ɓoye**:
  - Yana sanya `_hidden_` a farkon taken rukuni a cikin mashigin shafukan manhaja.
  - Yana ɓoye katunan rukunoni daga babban jerin shafuka, yana tura su zuwa `HiddenGroupsBar.svelte`.
  - Masu amfani za su iya bayyana ko dawo da rukunoni nan take da dannawa ɗaya.

### 3.3 Ayyukan Rukunoni da Sarrafa Ma'ajiya
- **Warware Rukuni (Ungroup)**: Yana warware rukunin, yana barin shafukan a buɗe a cikin taga.
- **Rufe Rukuni**: Yana rufe duk shafukan da ke cikin rukunin tare da sakin lambar ID ta rukunin.
- **Rufe Sauran Rukunoni**: Yana rufe duk rukunonin shafuka ban da rukunin da aka mai da hankali a kansa a halin yanzu.
- **Sake Rarraba Duk Shafuka (`Alt+Shift+R`)**: Yana gudanar da cikakken bincike a kan ƙa'idoji na musamman, rukunonin tsarin kwamfuta, da tattara yankuna.
- **Ɗaukar Hoton Dukkan Shafukan Rukuni**: Yana bi shafukan rukunin ɗaya bayan ɗaya yana ɗaukar hotonsu (`Cs` wanda ake gani, `Cp` cikakken shafi, `CP` sassan cikakken shafi).

### 3.4 Tsarin Ajiye Kwafi da Maido da Rukunoni
- **Ma'ajiyar `backupsGroups` a IndexedDB**:
  - Yana ajiye kwafin rukunin shafi ɗaya ko duk rukunonin da ke buɗe a wurin aiki a cikin ma'ajiyar IndexedDB `Intelligent_Workspace` mai suna `backupsGroups`.
  - Yana ɗaukar cikakken tsarin rukuni: taken rukuni, launi, yanayin naɗewa, taken shafuka, URLs, alamun favicon, matsayin maƙalawa, da wuraren shafuka.
  - **Kare Ma'ajiya**: Shafukan da aka ajiye kwafinsu ana rufe su cikin tsabta don dawo da ƙarfin RAM da rage nauyin CPU.
  - **Hanyoyin Mayarwa**:
    - Fuskar UI ta Kwafi a cikin `ListGroup.svelte` tare da maido da rukuni da dannawa ɗaya.
    - Gajerun hanyoyin maɓallai `br` (maido) da `bg` (ajiye kwafin duka).
    - Umarnin Omnibar `bgr:` (bincika da maido da rukunonin da aka ajiye kwafinsu) da `b:` (binciken alamun shafi).
  - **Fitarwa da Shigarwar JSON**: Cikakken fitarwa da shigar da kwafin JSON don sauƙin matsawa tsakanin kwamfutoci da adana bayanai na dogon lokaci ba tare da intanet ba.

### 3.5 Na'urar Kunna YouTube da Aka Ajiye a Wurin Rukunoni Masu Ɓoye
- **Tsarin Gine-gine (`HiddenGroupsBar.svelte`)**:
  - Lokacin da mai amfani ya fita daga shafin bidiyon YouTube ko ya buɗe bidiyo a cikin side panel, ba a rufe na'urar kunna bidiyon ko rasa ta. Ana ajiye ta ta atomatik a cikin `HiddenGroupsBar.svelte` a matsayin alamar YouTube da aka ajiye (`.youtube-indicator`).
  - Ana sarrafa ta ta hanyar ma'ajiyar `hiddenYoutubeView` a cikin `src/ui/stores/appStore.svelte.js`.
  - **Ayyuka da Dannawa Ɗaya**:
    - Danna alamar da aka ajiye yana kiran `restoreYoutubeView()`, yana mayar da na'urar kunna bidiyo nan take zuwa wurin aiki mai aiki.
    - Danna alamar gogewa yana kiran `discardYoutubeView()`, yana dakatar da kunna bidiyon cikin tsabta tare da dawo da kayan aiki.
  - Tana zama cikin sauƙi tare da rukunonin shafuka masu ɓoye a saman fuskar ListGroup ba tare da cika jerin shafuka masu aiki ba.

## 4. Sashe na 3: Kewayawa da Sarrafa Shafuka (Navegación)

Saurin kewayawa ta maɓallan kwamfuta da sarrafa wurin aiki suna ba da damar cikakken iko a kan shafuka da yawa ba tare da amfani da linzamin kwamfuta (mouse) ba.

### 4.1 Motsin Tsallaka Shafuka Cikin Sauri
- **Tsarin Umarni**: `Alt + [groupPrefix] + [tabIndex] + Enter`
- **Yadda Ake Yi**:
  - Riƙe `Alt` sannan ka rubuta sunan rukunin ko haruffan farko, sannan ka sanya lambar shafin da kake so (farawa daga 1), sannan ka danna `Enter`.
  - Misali: `Alt + git + 2 + Enter` yana tsallakawa kai tsaye zuwa shafi na biyu a cikin rukunin "GitHub".
  - Lambar `0` tana aiki ne a matsayin gajeriyar hanya zuwa shafi na *ƙarshe* a cikin rukunin.
  - Barin lambar yana tsallakawa ne zuwa shafi na *farko* a cikin rukunin.
  - Ana daidaita alamomin rubutu ta atomatik (`deaccent`) kuma bincike yana cire bambancin alamomi.

### 4.2 Maɓallan Takawa, Musayawa, da Sarrafa Rayuwar Shafi
- `s`: Musanya nan take zuwa shafin da ke aiki a baya (`previousActiveTabId`).
- `h` / `l`: Matsa zuwa shafin da ya gabata / na gaba (ko gungura gefe idan shafin ya ba da dama).
- `H` / `L`: Koma baya / ci gaba a tarihin manhaja.
- `t`: Buɗe sabon shafi marar komai.
- `x`: Rufe shafin da ke aiki a halin yanzu.
- `yt`: Yi kwafin shafin da ke aiki a halin yanzu.
- `r` / `R`: Sake loda shafi / Tilasta sake lodawa ba tare da amfani da cache ba.
- `d` / `u`: Gungura zuwa ƙasan / saman shafi.
- `j` / `k`: Gungura ƙasa / sama a hankali (matakin 75px).
- `f`: Kunna alamomin hanyoyin Vim (haruffa suna bayyana a kan abubuwan da za a iya dannawa).
- `cf`: Kunna alamomin hanyoyin Vim don kwafar URL zuwa allon kwafi (clipboard).

### Ga ƙwararrun masu amfani

Ƙwararrun masu amfani za su iya sanya manhajar bincike a yanayin cikakken allo (full-screen) kuma su kewaya gaba ɗaya ta hanyar maɓallan kwamfuta, ba tare da amfani da linzamin kwamfuta ko kaɗan ba.

#### Cikakken Shiga Ciki da Yin Bincike Ba Tare da Linzamin Kwamfuta Ba
- **Shiga/Fita Cikakken Allo**: Danna `F11` (Windows/Linux) ko `Command+Control+F` (macOS). Kayan aikin manhaja, mashigin adireshi, mashigin shafuka, da allunan tsarin kwamfuta za su ɓuya, suna barin 100% na allon don abun ciki kawai.
- **Kewayawa ta Hanyoyin Vim a Shafi (`f` / `cf`)**:
  - Danna `f` don samar da alamomin haruffa biyu masu launin rawaya a kan kowane abu da za a iya dannawa, maɓalli, wurin rubutu, da hanyar yanar gizo a cikin DOM.
  - Rubuta haruffa biyu da suka dace da inda kake son shiga don dannawa kai tsaye ba tare da linzamin kwamfuta ba.
  - Danna `cf` don kunna alamomin da ke kwafar URL na hanyar da aka nufa kai tsaye zuwa allon kwafin kwamfutarka.
  - Danna `vp` don buɗe ƙaramar taga ta duba hanyar da aka nufa a cikin shafi.
  - Danna `Esc` a kowane lokaci don soke alamomin hanyoyi da komawa gungurawa ta al'ada.
- **Tsallaka da Canza Shafuka Cikin Sauri**:
  - `Alt + [groupPrefix] + [tabIndex] + Enter`: Yi motsin tsallaka shafi cikin sauri (misali, `Alt + dev + 1 + Enter` yana tsallakawa kai tsaye zuwa shafi na farko a rukunin "Dev"; lamba `0` tana zaɓar shafi na ƙarshe a rukunin).
  - `s`: Musanya nan take tsakanin shafin yanzu da shafin da ya yi aiki a baya (`previousActiveTabId`).
  - `h` / `l`: Taka a jere zuwa shafin da ya gabata / na gaba a mashigin shafuka.
  - `H` / `L`: Matsa baya / gaba a cikin tarihin kewayawa na manhaja.
  - `t` / `x` / `yt`: Buɗe sabon shafi (`t`), rufe shafin yanzu (`x`), ko yi kwafin shafi (`yt`).
- **Tsarin Umarnin Omnibar Mai Yawo (`o`)**:
  - Danna `o` a kowane shafi don kiran Omnibar a tsakiyar allo.
  - Danna `@` don nuna cikakken jerin dukkan prefixes 41 na umarni.
  - Rubuta `f: <query>` don gudanar da cikakken binciken rubutu a duk shafukan da ke buɗe a duk tagogi.
  - Rubuta `sp:` don tura kewayawa kai tsaye zuwa kowace fuskar Sidepanel (`rules`, `listgroup`, `notes`, `gallery`, `gemini`, `activity`, `themes`, `hints`, `downloads`).
  - Rubuta `qai: <query>` ko `qaia: <task>` don tambayar Gemini ko ba wa wakilin manhaja umarni ba tare da cire hannu daga kan maɓallai ba.
  - Rubuta `ts:` don raba shafukan da ke buɗe su zama gefe-da-gefe.
- **Kewayawa Cikin Shafi da Mayar da Hankali**:
  - `j` / `k`: Gungura ƙasa / sama a hankali (ƙarin 75px).
  - `d` / `u`: Gungura rabin shafi ƙasa / sama.
  - `i`: Mayar da maƙallin rubutu (cursor) nan take zuwa babban filin rubutu a shafin.
  - `ESC`: Cire mayar da hankali daga filin rubutu da komawa yanayin kewayawa.

### 4.3 Sarrafa Sauti da Saututtuka
- Shafukan da ke kunna sauti suna nuna alamar lasifika a cikin ListGroup UI.
- `st`: Kunna ko kashe sautin shafin da ke aiki a halin yanzu.
- `so`: Kashe sautin duk shafuka masu sauti a cikin dukkan manhajar bincike.
- Zaɓin danna dama: "Silenciar Todas las Pestañas" / "Reactivar Sonido en Todas las Pestañas".

### 4.4 Hanyoyin Raba Fuska
- **Kunnawa**: Alamar Vim `ts` ko prefix na Omnibar `ts:`.
- **Aiki**: Yana amfani da `chrome.system.display` don lissafin girman allo. Yana raba shafuka biyu gefe-da-gefe zuwa tagogi biyu da ke kusa da juna, yana haɗa su a ƙarƙashin rukunin shafuka na musamman mai suna `Split`.
- **Maidowa**: Rufe tagar da aka raba ko sauya yanayin raba fuska yana mayar da girman taga da wurinta na asali.

### 4.5 Tsarin Maimaita Bidiyon YouTube
Tsarin maimaita bidiyon YouTube yana ba da ingantaccen iko na maimaita faifan bidiyo wanda aka haɗa kai tsaye a cikin na'urar kunna bidiyo ta YouTube:
- **Maɓallin Na'urar Kwamfuta (`#itg-yt-loop-button`)**: An sanya shi a cikin mashigin sarrafa na'urar YouTube (`.ytp-right-controls`), a gefen hagu na maɓallin Cast / Remote ko na buɗe PiP. Yana ɗauke da hoton SVG mai motsi, launin nunawa (`ytp-loop-active`, `var(--interactive-color, #ff4444)`), da yanayin `aria-pressed`.
- **Maɓallin Shorts (`#itg-yt-shorts-loop-button`)**: An sanya shi a ciki `#itg-yt-shorts-loop-wrapper` a shafi na hannun dama na YouTube Shorts (`ytd-shorts`), yana ba da damar maimaita gajerun bidiyoyi da dannawa ɗaya.
- **Zaɓuɓɓukan Maimaitawa da Tagar PiP (`#itg-yt-loop-menu` da `#itg-pip-loop-popup`)**: Tsayawa a kai ko danna maɓallin maimaitawa yana buɗe cikakken menu na saitunan maimaitawa:
  - **Maimaita A-B**: Masu amfani za su iya saita wurin farawa (A) da wurin ƙarewa (B) har zuwa matakin millisecond tare da sandunan zamewa da filayen saka lokaci na hannu.
  - **Yanayin Zagaye da Adadin Maimaitawa**: Saita adadin maimaita takamaiman sashe ko kunna yanayin maimaitawa marar iyaka (`itg-loop-seq-inf`).
  - **Jerin Maimaitawa da Jerin Sassa Masu Yawa**: Jeranta sassa da yawa na A-B waɗanda za su riƙa tashi a jere bisa adadin da aka saita.
  - **Nuna Hali da Tsawon Lokaci**: Yana nuna matsayin bidiyon a cikin sashen da ake ciki da kuma jimillar tsawon lokacin bidiyon (`00:00 / 00:00`).
  - **Babban Maɓalli (`youtubeLoopEnabled`)**: Ana adana shi a cikin `chrome.storage.sync` da `local`, yana ba masu amfani damar kunna ko kashe maɓallan maimaitawa a YouTube da YouTube Shorts ta shafin Customize Hints (`YoutubeLoopSection.svelte`).

### 4.6 Sautin Duba YouTube Shorts
- **Dabarar Buɗe Sauti (`youtubePreviewAudioHook.js`)**:
  - Yana aiki a duniyar `MAIN` don samun damar shiga kaddarar `#inline-preview-player` a kan abubuwan `ytd-video-preview`.
  - **Shawo Kan Tilasta Kashe Sauti**: YouTube yana tilasta kashe sauti a kan duban Shorts ta hanyar share kayan sarrafa sauti da mayar da `muted = true` ta hanyar masu sauraron `volumechange` na ciki.
  - Wannan ƙarin manhaja yana tare kaddarar `HTMLMediaElement.prototype.muted` a kan asalin `<video>` lokacin da linzamin kwamfuta ya tsaya a kai. Yana hana sake kashe sauti da YouTube ke tilastawa yayin da yake barin mai amfani ya kunna sauti, yana tabbatar da cewa sauti yana tashi ba tare da katsewa ba har sai linzamin kwamfuta ya fita.

### 4.7 Tsarin Hoto-cikin-Hoto (PiP)
- **Hoto-cikin-Hoto na Takarda (`wp`)**:
  - Yana amfani da sabuwar hanyar `documentPictureInPicture` API don buɗe cikakken shafin yanar gizo ko guntun rubutu a cikin ƙaramar taga mai yawo a saman komai.
  - Yana daidaita lokacin bidiyo ta atomatik lokacin canja wurin kallo.
  - Yana da tsarin sake gwadawa (`itgRetryIfPipFrameRefused`) kuma yana share bayanan kai na DNR iframe idan an rufe.
- **Hoto-cikin-Hoto na Bidiyo (`wv`)**:
  - Yana tare `HTMLVideoElement.prototype.requestPictureInPicture` ta hanyar `videoPipHook.js`.
  - Yana tura na'urorin kunna bidiyo zuwa fuskokin PiP na musamman tare da kayan sarrafa maimaitawa da zaɓin saurin kallo.
- **Auto-PiP (Kunnawa ta Automatik)**:
  - `as`: Kunna/kashe Auto-PiP yayin gungurawa (yana buɗe bidiyo a PiP idan ya fita daga allon kallo).
  - `ah`: Kunna/kashe Auto-PiP yayin ɓoye shafi (yana buɗe bidiyo a PiP idan aka canza zuwa wani shafin).

### 4.8 Hanyoyin Karanta Shafi da Sauye-sauyen Bayyanar Gani
- **Launukan Tacewar CSS**: Yana sanya salo a cikin shafukan yanar gizo ba tare da ɓata su ba:
  - `mb` / `mB`: Baƙi (Babban Bambancin Duhu) — fannin shafi / fannin duniya baki ɗaya.
  - `ms` / `mS`: Sepia (Takarda Mai Dumi) — fannin shafi / fannin duniya baki ɗaya.
  - `mp` / `mP`: Takarda (Fari da Baƙi Zalla) — fannin shafi / fannin duniya baki ɗaya.
  - `ml` / `mL`: Haske (Fari Mai Taushi) — fannin shafi / fannin duniya baki ɗaya.
  - `me` / `mE`: Tacewar CSS ta musamman da mai amfani ya tsara — fannin shafi / fannin duniya baki ɗaya.
  - `ESC`: Yana cire salon tacewar shafi nan take.
- **Yanayin Karatu da Karanta da Murya (TTS)**:
  - `fetchContentForReaderView`: Yana ciro sahihin abun cikin maƙala, yana cire tallace-tallace, shafukan gefe, da abubuwan ɗauke hankali na kewayawa.
  - Injin karanta rubutu da murya (TTS) da ke karanta rubutu jumla-jumla da babbar murya.
  - Kayan sarrafa kewayawa na maɓallai: `zp` (kunna/tsayar), `zn` (jumla ta gaba), `zb` (jumla ta baya), `zq` (rufe yanayin karatu).

### 4.9 Tsarin Gajerun Rubuce-rubuce tare da Sauye-sauyen Samfuri
Tsarin Gajerun Rubuce-rubuce (`SnippetManager` a cikin `src/utils/hint/snippets.js` da `src/utils/snippet-panel.js`) yana saurin rubutu ta hanyar buɗe gajerun kalmomi a cikin kowane filin shigarwa, textarea, ko babban edita:

- **Sauye-sauyen Samfuri (Template Variables)**: Yana mayar da sauye-sauye zuwa ainihin bayansu a lokacin buɗewa:
  - `{{date}}` / `date`: Kwanan wata na yanki (`toLocaleDateString`).
  - `{{isodate}}` / `isodate`: Kwanan wata na ISO 8601 (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
  - `{{longdate}}` / `longdate`: Dogon kwanan wata tare da ranar mako, rana, wata, da shekara.
  - `{{time}}` / `time`: Lokaci mai lamba biyu (`HH:MM`).
  - `{{datetime}}` / `datetime`: Haɗin kwanan wata da lokaci.
  - `{{weekday}}` / `weekday`: Cikakken sunan rana (misali, Litinin).
  - `{{day}}` / `day`: Ranar wata (1-31).
  - `{{month}}` / `month`: Cikakken sunan wata (misali, Oktoba).
  - `{{year}}` / `year`: Cikakkiyar shekara mai lamba 4.
  - `{{tomorrow}}` / `tomorrow`: Kwanan watan gobe (+rana 1).
  - `{{yesterday}}` / `yesterday`: Kwanan watan jiya (-rana 1).
  - `{{nextweek}}` / `nextweek`: Kwanan watan mako mai zuwa (+kwanaki 7).
  - `{{timestamp}}` / `timestamp`: Lokacin millisecond na zamani (`Date.now()`).
  - `{{url}}` / `url`: URL na shafin da ke aiki (`window.location.href`).
  - `{{domain}}` / `domain`: Sunan masaukin yanki mai aiki (`window.location.hostname`).
  - `{{pagetitle}}` / `pagetitle`: Taken shafin da ke aiki (`document.title`).
  - `{{uuid}}` / `uuid`: Lambar shaida ta musamman mai tsaro (`crypto.randomUUID()`).

## 5. Sashe na 4: Mataimakin AI da Wakili Mai Cin Gashin Kansa (Asistente AI)

Mataimakin AI yana haɗa manyan samfuran harshe (LLMs) a cikin manhaja don samar da fahimtar mahallin aiki, ayyuka masu sarrafa kansu, da kuma cikakken iko a kan manhajar bincike.

### 5.1 Tsarin Injunan Ƙirar Biyu
1. **Google Gemini Cloud API (`gemini-api.js`)**:

## 6. Sashe na 5: Duba Bayanan Kula da Gudanar da Ilimi (Ver Notas)

Injin ɗaukar bayanan kula wanda ke aiki a cikin na'ura da farko kuma yake la'akari da mahallin kewayawa da rukunonin shafukan manhaja.

### 6.1 Tsarin Ma'ajiya (`notesStore`)
Ana adana shi a cikin ma'ajiyar bayanai ta IndexedDB `Intelligent_Workspace` (v8) a cikin rumbun adana abubuwa na `notesStore`:
```typescript
interface NoteRecord {
  id: string;          // Lambar shaidar bayanin kula ta musamman.
  title: string;       // Taken bayanin kula.
  text: string;        // Cikakken rubutun HTML, Markdown, ko tsarin aiki da aka adana.

}
```

### 6.2 Tsarukan Bayanan Kula na Musamman da Ƙarin Nuni
1. **Bayanin Kula na Rubutu (`NoteTextEditor.svelte`)**:

- **Canza Tsari Cikin Sauri Ba Tare da Asarar Bayanai Ba**: Bayanan kula suna iya canzawa tsakanin tsarin Rubutu, Lissafin Dubawa, da Kanban ba tare da rasa wani bayani ba.

## 7. Sashe na 6: Duba Kafofin Watsa Labarai da Rumbun Hoto (Ver Galería)

### 7.1 Hanyoyin Ɗauka da Fasaha (Dukkan Nau'o'in Ɗauka 7)
Wannan ƙarin manhaja yana aiwatar da hanyoyin ɗauka na musamman guda 7 da suka shafi shafi guda, shafuka da yawa, yanki da aka yanke, da ayyukan launi:
1. **`visible` (Ɗaukar Yankin da Ake Gani - `cs`)**:
   - Yana ɗaukar ainihin abin da ke bayyane a kan shafin da ke aiki ta amfani da `chrome.tabs.captureVisibleTab`.

2. **`fullPage` (Ɗaukar Cikakken Shafin da Ake Gungurawa Cikin Santsi - `cp`)**:
   - Yana gudanar da gungura shafi ta atomatik, yana lissafin jimillar tsawon takardar.
   - Yana ɓoye sandunan gungurawa da mashigin kewayawa mai maƙale bayan ɓangaren farko don kawar da rikicewar gani.
   - Yana ɗinka sassan hotunan a kan `OffscreenCanvas` har zuwa tsawon 30,000px (`captureFullPageDataUrl`), yana bin ƙa'idar Chromium ta dakatawar 600ms tsakanin ɗaukar kowane hoto.
3. **`fullPageParts` (Yankakken Sassan Cikakken Shafi a Jere - `cpp` / `cP`)**:
   - Yana yanka dogayen shafuka zuwa sassa daban-daban masu tsawon allon kallo (`captureFullPageParts`), yana samar da hotuna har guda 60 masu lamba don sauƙin bibiya.
4. **`area` (Zaɓar Yanki ta Hanyar Jawo Murabba'i - `ca`)**:
   - Yana sanya `area-selector.js`, yana shimfiɗa allon zaɓe tare da nuna ainihin girman yankin a ainihin lokaci.
   - Hannun jan zaɓi, bayanin girman yanki, maɓallin tabbatarwa, soke aiki da maɓallin `Esc`, da layin jiran ɗaukar yankuna a shafuka da yawa (`handleCaptureAreaFromShortcut`).
5. **`groupTabs` (Ɗaukar Dukkan Shafukan Rukunin da Ke Aiki - `Cs`, `Cp`, `CP`)**:
   - Yana bi dukkan shafukan da ke cikin rukunin shafuka masu aiki ɗaya bayan ɗaya (`handleCaptureGroupFromShortcut`).
   - Yana goyon bayan ɗaukar hoto a jere a yanayin abin da ake gani, cikakken shafi, ko yankakken sassa, yana haɗa su wuri guda.
6. **`omnibarPickedTabs` (Ɗaukar Hoton Shafukan da Aka Zaɓa na Musamman)**:
   - Ana farawa ta hanyar umarnin Omnibar `cs:`, `cp:`, `cpp:`, ko `ca:` (`handleCaptureTabs`).
   - Yana ɗaukar hotunan shafukan da mai amfani ya tace ɗaya bayan ɗaya, yana nuna ci gaban aikin a ainihin lokaci.
7. **`colorPickerCapture` (Ɗaukar Hoton Bayan Fage don Zaɓar Launi)**:
   - Yana ɗaukar hoton bayan fage na allo don samar da madubin duba launi na hex a kan tsarin kwamfuta da ba su da hanyar `window.EyeDropper` ta asali (misali, Linux).

### 7.2 Fuskar Rumbun Hoto da Dorewa

## 8. Sashe na 7: Rukunin Pomodoro da Ƙididdigar Lokaci (Pomodoro)

Rukunin mai da hankali da auna lokaci wanda aka ƙera don ingantaccen aiki ba tare da kuskuren lokaci ba ko da manhaja ta shiga barci.

### 8.1 Injin Ƙididdigar Lokaci da Tsarin Bayan Fage
- **Lissafin Lokaci Marar Kuskure**: Ana adana halin da ake ciki a cikin `chrome.storage.local` tare da cikakkun lokutan Unix (`startTime`, `pausedAt`). Kashewar service worker, sake kunna manhaja, da yanayin barcin kwamfuta ba sa haifar da kuskuren lokaci ko kaɗan.
- **Mai Rarraba Ƙararrawa**:
  - Ƙididdigar saukar lokaci mai inganci da ke aiki ta `chrome.alarms` (`pomodoroTick` a kowane daƙiƙa 1).
  - Ayyukan gyare-gyaren bayan fage da share abubuwa na dogon lokaci da ke gudana ta `itg-periodic-tasks` (kowane minti 1).
- **Sautin Ƙararrawa a Wajen Allo (`offscreen.html`)**: Ana samar da sautin ƙararrawa ta hanyar takardar Chrome Offscreen ta musamman da aka ƙirƙira da dalilin `AUDIO_PLAYBACK`. Tana rufe kanta ta atomatik daƙiƙa 3.5 bayan kammala sauti don adana ƙarfin ma'ajiya.

### 8.2 Cikakkun Hanyoyin Ƙididdigar Lokaci 4
1. **Pomodoro (`pomodoro`)**:

   - Dogon hutu: mintuna 1 zuwa 180 (asali: mintuna 15).

   - Manufar adadin zagaye: zagaye 1 zuwa 99 (asali: 8).

2. **Auna Lokaci / Stopwatch (`cronometro`)**: Mai auna ci gaban lokaci tare da ɗaukar lokutan zagaye da damar tsayawa a matsayin cikakken tsayawa.
3. **Ƙidaya Baya / Countdown (`temporizador`)**: Mai ƙidayar saukar lokaci ta musamman da ke bayyana awoyi, mintuna, da daƙiƙoƙi.
4. **Ƙararrawar Takamaiman Lokaci (`tiempo`)**: Zaɓin kwanan wata a kalanda da zaɓin agogo don saita ƙararrawa a wani takamaiman lokaci na gaba.

### 8.3 Ƙaramin Na'urar Auna Lokaci Mai Yawo a Cikin Shafi

## 9. Sashe na 8: Allon Ayyuka da Tarihi (Panel de Actividad)

Injin bibiyar Declarative Net Request (DNR), kididdiga, da kula da dokokin amfani da yanar gizo.

### 9.1 Injin Bayanai da Yanayin Ma'ajiya (`ITG_WEB_ACTIVITY`)
Ana sarrafa shi a ƙarƙashin `ITG_WEB_ACTIVITY`:
- `wa:day:YYYY-MM-DD`: Ma'aunin yanki na kowace rana (`{ domains: { host: { t: seconds, v: visits, s: sessions, h: { hour: seconds } } } }`).
- `wa:days`: Babban jerin maɓallan kwanan wata da aka ɗauka.
- `wa:recent`: Ma'ajiyar madauwari da ke ɗauke da shigarwar tarihin ziyara 400 na baya-bayan nan.
- `wa:openSegment`: Sashen lokaci mai aiki a cikin `chrome.storage.session`, wanda aka iyakance a `WA_MAX_SEGMENT_SECONDS = 900s` (mintuna 15) don hana ɓata kididdiga idan kwamfuta ta shiga barci ba tare da tura saƙon rashin aiki ba.
- **Gano Rashin Aiki**: Yana amfani da `chrome.idle` (asali: 300s / 5m). Kunna sauti yana ci gaba da aikin bibiya koda kuwa ba a mai da hankali a kan tagogi ba idan `countAudible = true`.
- **Riƙe Bayanai**: Yana goge bayanan ranakun da suka wuce `retentionDays` ta atomatik (asali: kwanaki 180).

### 9.2 Mai Toshe Shafuka na Declarative Net Request (DNR)
- **Raba Ƙa'idoji Masu Canzawa**: Yana ba da lambobin ƙa'idoji masu canzawa a cikin kewayon ID `9000` zuwa `9999`.
- **Wurin Tura Shafin da Aka Toshe**: Shafukan da aka toshe ana tura su zuwa `src/ui/pages/web-activity/blocked.html?d=<domain>&u=\1`.
- **Share Shafukan da Ke Buɗe**: Lokacin da ƙa'idar lokacin shafi ta cika, ma'aikacin bayan fage yana duba tare da tura dukkan shafukan da ke buɗe masu dacewa da yankin nan take.
- **Kare Lokutan Dare**: Shirye-shiryen da suka ketare tsakar dare (misali, `22:00` zuwa `02:00`) ana duba su ta atomatik a kan ranaku biyu.
- **Lokacin Daga Ƙafa (+minti 5)**: Allon toshewar yana ba da izinin daga ƙafa na mintuna 5 na wucin gadi, wanda ake bibiya a cikin `wa:snoozeUses`.

### 9.3 Makullin Tsaro na Asymmetric (`blockLock.js`)
Ana iya kare iyakokin shafuka da makullin kalmar sirri na musamman:
- **Ma'ajiyar Hash**: Yana adana kalmar salt mai girman 16-byte da SHA-256 hex digest (`wa:settings.blockPassword`).
- **Dokar Ƙa'ida ta Asymmetric**: **Ba a taɓa tambayar** kalmar sirri lokacin ƙirƙirar sabbin iyakoki ko ƙara tsaurara ƙa'idoji (misali, rage lokacin da aka bayar ko ƙara ƙuntatawar lokaci).
- **Ayyukan da Aka Tsare**: Ana neman kalmar sirri ne kawai lokacin **sassauta ƙa'idoji** (goge iyaka, ƙara lokacin da aka bayar, ko kashe shirye-shiryen lokaci) ko lokacin neman daga ƙafa bayan an wuce iyakar kyauta ta yau da kullum (`snoozePasswordAfter`).

### 9.4 Rarraba Rukuni da Kididdigar Gani
- **Rukunoni 16 da Aka Gina a Ciki**: `social`, `video`, `music`, `news`, `shopping`, `reading`, `entertainment`, `dev`, `work`, `education`, `games`, `finance`, `mail`, `ai`, `search`, `other`.
- **Rukunoni na Musamman**: Yana goyon bayan rukunonin da mai amfani ya tsara masu ɗauke da prefix na `custom:`.
- **Ma'aunin Mai da Hankali**: Yana kwatanta rukunoni masu amfani (`dev`, `work`, `education`, `reading`, `finance`, `mail`) da rukunoni masu ɗauke hankali (`social`, `video`, `games`, `shopping`, `entertainment`).
- **Abubuwan Gani**: Rarraba ayyukan kowace sa'a ta Chart.js, zane mai siffar donut na rukunoni, da jerin darajojin yankuna.

### 9.5 Tsarin Sarrafa Saukakkun Fayiloli (`switchMainView('downloads')`)
- **Hanya ta Musamman**: Ana shiga ta side panel ta `switchMainView('downloads')` (`?view=downloads`).
- **Haɗin Chrome API Kai Tsaye**: Yana haɗuwa da `chrome.downloads` don bibiya, sarrafawa, da duba duk abubuwan da manhaja ke saukarwa a ainihin lokaci.
- **Bincike Mai Sauƙi a Ainihin Lokaci**: Bincike nan take ta sunan fayil, hanyar fayil, ko asalin URL da aka saukar da shi daga gare shi.
- **Alamomin Tace Hali**: Tace saukakkun fayiloli cikin sauri ta halinsu: `All`, `In Progress`, `Complete`, `Paused`, da `Interrupted`.
- **Tace ta Kwanan Watan Kalanda**: Kayyade saukakkun fayiloli ta takamaiman kwanan wata ko lokaci.
- **Mai Saukar da Fayilolin Rukuni a Tare**: Yana ciro duk hanyoyin saukar da fayiloli daga buɗaɗɗun shafukan da ke cikin rukunin da ke aiki kuma yana fara saukar da su a tare tare da nuna ci gaban aikin.
- **Sarrafa Fayil**: Buɗe fayil da dannawa ɗaya kai tsaye (`chrome.downloads.open`) da nuna folda (`chrome.downloads.show`).

### 9.6 Tsarin Shafukan da Aka Rufe Kwanan Nan da Tarihin Bincike
- **Fuskar Shafukan da Aka Rufe Kwanan Nan**:
  - An haɗa shi kai tsaye da `chrome.sessions.getRecentlyClosed`.
  - Bincike a ainihin lokaci tare da daidaita alamomin rubutu ta atomatik (`deaccent`).
  - Maido da shafi guda ɗaya da maido da dukkan zaman taga da dannawa ɗaya ko ta umarnin Omnibar `c:`.
- **Fuskar Tarihin Bincike**:
  - Zurfafa bincike tare da ma'ajiya mai ɗaukar daƙiƙa 10 (TTL) a cikin ƙwaƙwalwa don kawar da yawan tambayoyin `chrome.history.search`.
  - Bincike da za a iya tacewa ta yanki, kalma, da kwanan watan kalanda.
  - Kunnawa da dannawa ɗaya idan shafin yana buɗe, ko buɗe sabon shafi, wanda za a iya shiga ta Omnibar `h:`.

### 9.7 Tsarin Shirya Lokutan Launukan Fage (`ThemeScheduleModal.svelte`)
- **Shirya Launin Fage ta Automatik**:
  - Ana sarrafa shi ta `ThemeScheduleModal.svelte` a cikin fuskar Themes ta side panel (`?view=themes` / `pt`).
  - Yana goyon bayan canza launin fage ta atomatik bisa lokaci na rana (`HH:MM`) da ranakun mako da ke aiki (`[0..6]`).
  - Matsakaicin shirye-shiryen launin fage na duniya guda 7 (`MAX_GLOBAL_SCHEDULES = 7`).
  - **Komawa ta Automatik**: Yana komawa zuwa launin fage na asali na rana ko na wurin aiki a ƙayyadaddun sa'o'in kammalawa.
  - **Sanarwar Kwamfuta**: Yana bayar da sanarwa a kan allon kwamfuta lokacin da aka canza launin fage ta atomatik (`chrome.notifications`).
  - **Adanawa ta Hanyoyi Biyu**: Ana adana shi a cikin `chrome.storage.sync` (saitunan tafiya da su) da `chrome.storage.local` a ƙarƙashin `savedThemes` da `themeSchedules`.

### 9.8 Ƙaramar Fuskar Sidepanel
- **Hanyar Sidepanel (`?view=activity` / `pw`)**: Ginshiƙi mai faɗin 350px wanda aka tsara don lura a ainihin lokaci, mai ɗauke da alamomin zaɓar lokaci (`Today`, `7d`, `30d`, `All`) da sandunan nuna ci gaban iyakokin yankuna.

### 10.6 Tsarin Zaɓuɓɓukan Danna Dama (Dukkan Rukunoni 5 da Ayyuka 30+)
Zaɓuɓɓukan danna dama suna ba da cikakken tsarin rarraba ayyuka da aka tsara zuwa rukunoni 5 na aiki:
1. **Rukuni na 1: Ƙa'idoji da Zaɓuɓɓukan URL**:
   - Babban akwatin kunna/kashe ƙa'idoji ("Activar/Desactivar Todas las Reglas").
   - Maɓallan kowace ƙa'ida na kunna ko kashe ƙa'idoji na musamman cikin sauri.
   - "Abrir Todas las URLs": Yana buɗe duk adiresoshin URL da aka yi wa rajista a ƙa'idar da aka zaɓa.
   - Jerin kowane URL tare da nuna adadin shafukan da ke buɗe a ainihin lokaci `[N]`: Yana nuna adadin shafukan da ke buɗe masu dacewa da URL ɗin, yana ba da damar shiga URL ɗin da dannawa ɗaya, kuma yana ba da damar "Cerrar Pestañas para esta URL" don rufe duk shafukan da suka dace.
   - Ƙirƙirar Ƙa'ida Cikin Sauri: "Crear Regla para esta Página" -> "Añadir Raíz del Sitio" / "Añadir URL Completa".
   - Ƙara zuwa Ƙa'idar da Ake da Ita: "Añadir a Regla Existente" -> Zaɓi ƙa'ida -> "Añadir Raíz del Sitio" / "Añadir URL Completa".
2. **Rukuni na 2: Tsabtace Shafuka da Kayan Sarrafa Sauti**:
   - "Eliminar Pestañas Duplicadas [N]": Yana nuna adadin shafukan da aka gano suna da kwafi kuma yana rufe su a duk tagogi.
   - "Eliminar Pestañas por Dominio": Jerin da ke nuna dukkan yankuna tare da adadin shafukansu da ke buɗe `[N]`, yana ba da damar rufe shafuka bisa takamaiman yanki.
   - "Cerrar Todos Excepto el Grupo Activo": Yana barin rukunin da ake aiki a kansa a halin yanzu tare da rufe dukkan sauran rukunoni.
   - "Reagrupar Todas las Pestañas": Yana fara cikakken sake rarraba dukkan buɗaɗɗun shafuka a bayan fage.
   - "Silenciar Todas las Pestañas" / "Reactivar Sonido en Todas las Pestañas": Kayan kashewa da kunna sautin manhaja gaba ɗaya.
3. **Rukuni na 3: Karatu, Zaɓe, da Bayanan Kula**:
   - "Leer Página en Voz Alta" (TTS): Karanta cikakken shafi da babbar murya tare da kayan sarrafawa a allo.
   - "Leer Selección en Voz Alta": Karanta rubutun da aka zaɓa a DOM da babbar murya ta amfani da fasahar murya.
   - "Crear Nota desde Selección": Yana ciro zaɓaɓɓen rubutu, ya samo taken bayanin kula ta atomatik daga kanun labarai mafi kusa (`h1`-`h3`), kuma ya adana shi a cikin `notesStore`.
4. **Rukuni na 4: Buɗe Ƙarin Manhaja, PiP, da Fuskoki**:
   - "Abrir como PiP" / "Abrir como Ventana Emergente": Buɗe shafin da aka nufa, rubutun da aka zaɓa, ko hoton da aka nufa a cikin Hoto-cikin-Hoto mai yawo ko popup mai zaman kansa.
   - "Abrir Extensión": Yana buɗe popup na ƙarin manhaja ko faɗaɗa side panel.
   - "Gestionar Reglas": Yana buɗe cikakken Mai Gudanar da Ƙa'idoji a wani shafin manhaja na daban.
   - Mabuɗan Sidepanel Kai Tsaye: Zuwa List Groups (`?view=listgroup`), Themes (`?view=themes`), Mataimakin AI (`?view=gemini`), da Customize Hints (`?view=hints`) da dannawa ɗaya.
5. **Rukuni na 5: Saituna da Zaɓuɓɓukan Tattara Shafuka**:
   - **Gestión de Grupos**: Alternar Grupo Actual (naɗe/buɗe rukunin yanzu), Alternar Todos los Grupos (naɗe/buɗe dukkan rukunoni).
   - **Opciones de Agrupación**:
     - Agrupación Inteligente (kunna/kashe tattara shafuka ta atomatik).
     - Modo Compacto (kunna/kashe yanayin sunan rukuni mai harafi ɗaya).
     - Dominio / Subdominio (kunna/kashe tattara yankuna da ƙananan yankuna).
     - Páginas Especiales: Shafukan Chrome (`chrome://`), Fayilolin Gida (`file:///`), Ƙarin Manhajoji (`chrome-extension://`), Sauran Abubuwa (`Misc`), Adiresoshin IP da Localhost.
   - **Opciones Generales**:
     - Ordenar Alfabéticamente (tilasta tsara shafuka bisa haruffan haruffa).
     - Ubicación Miscelánea: Farko (`start`), Ƙarshe (`end`), Haruffan Haruffa (`alpha`).
     - Temporizador de Auto-Colapso (kunna/kashe lokacin naɗe rukunoni marasa aiki).
     - Prefijos Visuales (kunna/kashe alamomin hali na `🔒`, `🔍`, `🗝️`, `⚠️`).
     - Vista Previa de Enlaces (kunna/kashe tagar duba hanyar yanar gizo a cikin shafi).

## 11. Tsarin Ma'ajiya da Adana Bayanai

Wannan ƙarin manhaja yana amfani da tsarin ma'ajiya mai hawa daban-daban a cikin IndexedDB da Chrome Storage APIs:

### 11.1 Ma'ajiyar Bayanai ta IndexedDB: `Intelligent_Workspace` (Tsari Na 8)
1. `screenshots`: keyPath: `'id'`, autoIncrement: true. Wurin duba na biyu: `'createdAt'`. Yana adana hotunan allo na binary, URLs na canvas data, alamomin maƙalawa na dindindin, girman hoto, da lambobin sassan hoto.
2. `geminiConversations`: keyPath: `'id'`, autoIncrement: true. Ba shi da wurin duba na biyu (ana sarrafa shi ta ID). Yana adana zaren tattaunawa na dogon lokaci, matsayin saƙonni, ma'aunin token, da bayanan kafa hujja da bincike.
3. `notesStore`: keyPath: `'id'`, autoIncrement: false. Ba shi da wurin duba na biyu (ana tace shi a ƙwaƙwalwa ta contextKey). Yana adana cikakken rubutu, jerin lissafin duba aiki, katunan Kanban, bayanan Mermaid, da dabarun KaTeX.
4. `backupsGroups`: keyPath: `'group.id'`. Ba shi da wurin duba na biyu (yana adana abubuwan kwafin rukuni ta hanyar `'group.id'`). Yana adana tsarin rukunoni da aka matse, launuka, sunaye, da bayanan shafukan da aka rufe.
5. `pomodoroStats`: keyPath: `'id'`, autoIncrement: true. Wuraren duba na biyu: `'projectName'` (keyPath `'projectName'`) da `'savedAt'` (keyPath `'savedAt'`) (babu na `date`). Yana adana kididdigar mai da hankali, ma'aunin zagaye, da tarihin katsewar aiki.

### 11.2 Rarraba Hanyoyin Ma'ajiya Biyu na Chrome (`StorageService`)
- Yana tura karantawa da rubutawa cikin sauƙi tsakanin `chrome.storage.sync` (don saitunan da za a iya amfani da su a duk na'urorin mai amfani) da `chrome.storage.local` (don manyan abubuwan da aka fi so).
- Muhimman abubuwan ciki:
  - `customRules`: Jerin ma'anar ƙa'idoji (`[{ name, color, active, urls }]`).
  - `clusterConfig`: Saitunan tattara shafuka ta atomatik (adadi, ƙaramar fuska, rukunoni na musamman).
  - `userPrefixes`: Alamomin emoji da aka saita (`{ lock, openKey, loupe, checked, warning }`).
  - `activeTheme`, `savedThemes`, da `themeSchedules`: Tsarin launuka 9 da ƙa'idojin canza su a kan lokaci da aka saita ta `ThemeScheduleModal.svelte`.
  - `pomodoroState`: Cikakkun lokutan zama na ainihin lokaci, tsawon lokacin dakatawa, da manufofin zagaye.
  - `wa:settings` da `wa:limits`: Saitunan bibiyar ayyukan yanar gizo, iyakokin shafuka, da lokutan aiki.
  - `youtubeLoopEnabled`: Alamar gaskiya ko ƙarya da ke sarrafa maɓallan maimaitawa a YouTube da Shorts.

### 11.3 Ma'ajiyar Zama ta Chrome (`chrome.storage.session`)
Ana amfani da ita don daidaita ayyukan wucin gadi cikin matuƙar sauri:
- `tabsEverActive`: Jerin lambobin ID na shafukan da suka taɓa yin aiki a zaman manhaja na yanzu.
- `groupExpandedEver`: Bayanan rukunonin da aka taɓa buɗewa a zaman manhaja na yanzu.
- `groupInfoMap`: Rajistar ainihin lokaci da ke haɗa lambobin ID na rukunoni da sunaye masu canzawa da nau'o'in tattara shafuka.
- `splitScreenState`: Girman taga da aka adana don maido da asalin girman allo.
- `contextMenusSignature`: Shaidar hash da ke hana sake gina menu na danna dama akai-akai yayin sauya shafuka cikin sauri.
- `wa:openSegment`: Sashen lokaci na gidan yanar gizon da ke aiki a halin yanzu.
- `hiddenYoutubeView`: Hanyar na'urar kunna YouTube da aka ajiye a cikin `HiddenGroupsBar.svelte`.

## 12. Fasahar Tsaron Kariya da Magance Matsalolin Gefe

1. **Cire Toshewar Bayanan Kai ta Declarative Net Request (`dnr.js`)**:
   - Yana canza bayanan martaba na kai ta hanyar ƙa'idojin DNR masu canzawa don cire `X-Frame-Options` da ƙuntatawar `Content-Security-Policy`, yana ba wa shafukan waje damar bayyana a cikin Side Panel da fuskokin Picture-in-Picture iframes.
   - **Tsaron WhatsApp Web**: Yana barin `Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy` (COOP) da `Cross-Origin-Embedder-Policy` (COEP) a bayyane a kan `web.whatsapp.com` don guje wa lalata tsarin ɓoye bayanan sirri na WebAssembly Signal.
2. **Kariyar Danna Dama da Zaɓen Rubutu (`allowRightClickHook.js`)**:
   - Yana shawo kan shafukan yanar gizo da ke toshe kwafi, liƙawa, menu na danna dama, da zaɓar rubutu.
   - Yana aiki a duniyar `MAIN` don tare `preventDefault()` da `stopPropagation()` a kan `contextmenu`, `copy`, `cut`, `selectstart`, `mousedown`, da `mouseup`.
   - Yana cire layukan `oncontextmenu="return false"` ta hanyar `MutationObserver`.
   - Yana kare sahihan manhajojin yanar gizo (misali, Telegram Web, Google Docs) ta hanyar duba abubuwan da ke cikin allon kwafi kafin sake sanya abubuwan liƙawa da aka soke.
3. **Kawar da Tilasta Kashe Sautin Duba YouTube Shorts (`youtubePreviewAudioHook.js`)**:
   - Yana aiki a duniyar `MAIN` a kan `ytd-video-preview #inline-preview-player`.
   - Yana sauya kaddarar `HTMLMediaElement.prototype.muted` a kan asalin `<video>` lokacin da linzamin kwamfuta ya tsaya a kai.
   - Yana kawar da matsalar gasar `volumechange` ta ciki ta YouTube da ke tilasta kashe sauti, yana kiyaye kunna sauti yayin da bidiyo ke maimaitawa muddin linzamin kwamfuta yana kai.
4. **Dabarar Kewayawa a Jerin YouTube Shorts Feed (`youtubeShortsFeedHook.js`)**:
   - Yana aiki a duniyar `MAIN` a kan `ytd-shorts`.
   - Yana tare kaddarar `shortsProcessedSequence`, wacce ba a iya gani daga fannin ƙarin manhaja mai zaman kansa, kuma yana tura al'amuran `yt-navigate` na asali a kan `ytd-app` ba tare da sake loda shafin ba ko rasa yanayin tagar da ke yawo.
5. **Kariyar Allon Zane na Shafuka Marasa Ƙarshe**:
   - Yana iyakance ɗaukar cikakken shafi zuwa sassa 60 kawai (`MAX_FULL_PAGE_SEGMENTS`) da matsakaicin tsawon allon zane na 30,000px (`MAX_FULL_PAGE_CANVAS_PX`), yana hana rushewar shafin manhaja saboda ƙarewar ma'ajiya.
6. **Madadin Zaɓar Launi a Kan Allon Linux**:
   - Lokacin aiki a kan tsarin Linux wanda ba shi da Chromium `window.EyeDropper` API, ƙarin manhajar yana sanya `screen-color-picker.js`, yana nuna madubin zuƙo pixel sau 9 tare da lissafin RGB/HEX/HSL.
7. **Jinkirin Dakatawa Lokacin Fara Zama**:
   - Yana jinkirta gyaran rukunoni na daƙiƙa 20 (`groupsHaveSettledAfterStartup()`) bayan buɗe manhaja don ba wa shafukan zama da yanayin tagogi damar daidaita kansu kafin a tantance rarraba rukunoni.
